In the Mood for Love

Wong Kar-wai - 2000 - 98 min

For the quiet, aching hour you described. Nothing is said outright; everything is felt.

Tokyo Story

Yasujiro Ozu - 1953 - 136 min

Slow, generous, and quietly devastating. A Sunday film in the truest sense.

Perfect Days

Wim Wenders - 2023 - 124 min

A life of small routines, handled with unusual care. Good for when you want calm, not plot.
